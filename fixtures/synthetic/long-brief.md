# Northern Pacific Coastal Refillable Personal Care Cooperative of Portland project brief

Northern Pacific Coastal Refillable Personal Care Cooperative of Portland is a fictional member-owned cooperative of independent refill shops preparing a regional launch across several neighborhoods with very different customer expectations and existing local partnerships.

The client wants a regional audience hypothesis covering urban and suburban shoppers, messaging directions that work for each member store without diluting the cooperative brand, a six-week launch content calendar coordinated across member stores, a partner and community outreach plan, a simple measurement plan with agreed definitions, and a short training guide that store staff can use before the launch begins. The authorized strategy budget is USD 14,500; paid media is separate.

Constraints: the engagement lasts six weeks, member stores have no shared customer data, and the cooperative board has not approved any sales targets, so the proposal must not promise growth.

Requested deliverables: discovery interviews, audience and message strategy, a coordinated content calendar, partner outreach planning, a measurement plan, and a staff training guide.
