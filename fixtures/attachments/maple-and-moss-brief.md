# Maple & Moss project brief

Maple & Moss is a fictional local refillable personal-care brand preparing a local launch.

The client wants a clear target-audience hypothesis, messaging directions, a four-week launch content calendar, and a simple measurement plan. The authorized strategy budget is USD 6,000; paid media is separate.

Constraints: the engagement lasts four weeks, there is no existing reliable campaign baseline, and the team does not have permission to promise sales growth. Proposed audience segments must be presented as hypotheses rather than demographic facts.

Requested deliverables match Harbor Creative's standard package: discovery, audience and message strategy, a four-week content calendar, and a measurement plan.
